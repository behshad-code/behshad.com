How it works

One question. Two sides. Your call.

Choose an item on the ballot, read the verified effects and candidate records, and use the source links to investigate further. You can adjust which issues are highlighted, without the app deciding what you should believe.

1. Ballot and options appear on the left.

2. Research, costs, tradeoffs and official ⓘ sources appear on the right.

3. Your choices are saved privately in this browser.

4. Print a reference sheet to help complete your real ballot.

This preview includes all 14 statewide propositions, the governor race and an example congressional district. It is not a verified address-specific full ballot, does not cast votes, and does not provide live AI answers.
