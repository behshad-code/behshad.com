About the publication

Useful health education, with the evidence and its limits in view.

Why Men's Health Compass exists, who publishes it, and how its articles are developed and maintained.

Men's Health Compass is a free educational publication hosted on behshad.com. It is designed to help men understand health topics and prepare more informed conversations with qualified healthcare professionals. Prostate health and prostate cancer are its first areas of coverage.

Publication and editorial responsibility

Men's Health Compass is responsible for selecting, researching, updating, and publishing its educational content. It is not a medical practice, healthcare provider, professional medical society, or a substitute for clinical care. The medical articles have not been independently peer reviewed by a licensed clinician. No editorial committee or clinical advisory board is represented or implied.

The illustrated guide figure sometimes shown in a white coat is fictional visual branding. It does not represent a physician, clinical qualification, endorsement, or professional medical review.

How information is developed

Topics are organized around questions people may face, such as interpreting PSA results, preparing for testing, comparing treatment choices, or living with treatment effects. We prioritize original clinical guidelines, published research, official regulatory notices, and established medical institutions. News stories and curated feeds may identify topics but are not, by themselves, sufficient evidence for a medical claim.

AI tools may assist with organizing research, drafting plain-language explanations, and technical production. AI-generated text is not clinical validation. We aim to check consequential claims against original public sources, identify populations and limitations, and distinguish established care from preliminary findings. Source access and verification limits are disclosed rather than hidden.

For details, read our editorial methods and the source library.

Review dates, updates and corrections

Dates indicate the latest editorial revision or evidence-check milestone described on a page, not a promise of continuous medical monitoring. Medical guidelines, regulatory indications, and trial findings can change. Important content updates are reviewed and versioned rather than silently replacing earlier explanations.

Our goal is to correct material errors, preserve context, and link readers to the authoritative source. Readers should check current guidance with their healthcare team before making decisions.

Privacy and future funding

Readers can explore the guides without creating an account. The site uses GoatCounter for aggregate visit and referral analytics. The searchable reference list and appointment-question builder operate within the browser and do not send the questions people select to us. We do not ask visitors to submit medical histories, diagnoses, or test results. See the privacy notice.

The core health guides remain free. The project may eventually use clearly identified sponsorships or affiliate recommendations to help support publishing costs. When a paid relationship exists, it will be disclosed near the relevant recommendation, with editorial judgments kept separate from compensation. No product purchase is required to read the health guides.

What this site cannot do

It cannot diagnose a condition, interpret an individual's tests, recommend a personal treatment, or replace advice from a licensed healthcare professional. If you have symptoms or a health concern, consult a qualified healthcare professional. Medical emergencies require urgent medical attention.
