Privacy at Men's Health Compass

How our health-education pages handle visits, educational tools, and external links.

Men's Health Compass is a public health-education publication hosted within behshad.com. You can read our guides without creating an account or providing health information. We do not offer diagnosis or individualized medical advice.

What we measure

We use GoatCounter for privacy-conscious aggregate page-view and referral analytics. This helps us see which public pages are visited and how readers arrive. We do not intentionally create identifiable visitor health profiles or send individual medical questions, health conditions, PSA values, or treatment decisions to our analytics system.

Website hosting and analytics services may process technical request information to deliver and operate their services. This notice does not promise that all web requests are anonymous or that third-party services never process IP addresses.

What stays in your browser

When you filter the medical reference library or use the appointment-question builder, filtering and question selection happen locally in your browser. We do not upload or store those entries. Please do not submit sensitive personal medical information through general site contact channels.

Links to other organizations

Research references, medical institutions, and external services have their own websites and privacy practices. When you follow an external link, that organization may receive ordinary browser request information and may use its own cookies or analytics.

Advertising and affiliate links

Men's Health Compass currently has no display advertising or advertising pixels. If we later add a commercial or affiliate link, the relationship will be disclosed near the link, and any material changes to visitor tracking will be reviewed and disclosed before activation. Reading medical education will not require a purchase.

Questions and updates

This page summarizes privacy practices specific to the health publication. The broader behshad.com sitewide privacy notice provides additional hosting and information-handling details. You can use the main site's contact section for general website inquiries. This notice will be updated if our information practices materially change.
