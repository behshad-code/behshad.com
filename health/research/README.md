# Prostate Health editorial research register

This is an internal-in-the-workflow but **public-repository** record of source leads for Men's Health Compass. It contains only public article titles, URLs, topic classifications, and verification status. It is not a library of republished articles.

## Purpose

Use a large source pool without turning the reader-facing website into a long newsfeed. The six published educational guides are the durable explanation layer; short selected research snapshots are a carefully filtered, dated layer.

## Intake rules

1. Record a source by original publisher URL, title, publication date when verified, and topic; keep Flipboard magazine URLs as provenance.
2. Identify repeated reporting of the same underlying study. Consolidate evidence, not just identical headlines.
3. Find the original study, journal, clinical trial, institutional announcement, guideline, or FDA decision before using a news report as support for a medical statement.
4. Note population, design, comparison group, clinical endpoint, limitations, and whether findings are preclinical, observational, clinical evidence, recommendations, or regulatory actions.
5. Separate findings that are **promising but not clinically actionable** from established practice. Don't imply a laboratory finding is an available diagnostic test.
6. Update a published guide only where the finding changes an explanatory claim or practical question; version any changed guide bodies in `content-versions/` and `CHANGELOG.md`.
7. For short research highlights, show an exact month/year, plain-language takeaway, evidence-status qualifier, and primary source link. Require manual editorial review before publishing.
8. Do not copy full articles or licensed guideline bodies. Use original prose, short attributed summaries, and links; respect publisher access restrictions.
9. Do not publish personal patient histories, unique results, medical records, or individual medical experiences from private sources.
10. Preserve existing article URLs and topic navigation. Do not generate one public page for each headline.

## Evidence tiers

- **Guideline/regulatory:** Published AUA or EAU guidance, FDA action, official NCI/NIDDK patient material.
- **Primary clinical study:** Original peer-reviewed trial/cohort with clear clinical population and limitations.
- **Preliminary:** Observational associations, mechanistic work, conference reports, pilot findings, news announcements.
- **Unverified lead:** Publisher news report where the underlying evidence has not yet been examined.
- **Excluded / duplicate:** Not specific enough to prostate health, outdated/retracted, or multiple reports of same finding.
