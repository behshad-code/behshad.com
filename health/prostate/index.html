A quick introduction

First, know what the prostate does.

The prostate is a small gland just below the bladder. It adds fluid to semen and surrounds part of the tube that carries urine out of the body. Changes in the prostate do not automatically mean cancer.

Three common prostate conditions, explained +

Enlargement (BPH)

Benign prostatic hyperplasia is a noncancerous enlargement that can make urination difficult, especially with age.

Prostatitis

Inflammation of the prostate can cause discomfort or urinary symptoms. Some cases involve infection; others do not.

Prostate cancer

Prostate cancer is a different condition. It may cause no symptoms early on, and urinary symptoms alone cannot diagnose it.

Research & updates

What's changing in prostate health?

Research brings new possibilities, but a promising study isn't the same as an approved test or treatment. These selected developments show what the evidence says today.

October 2026 · Early research

Could a blood test reveal future risk?

Oxford researchers found blood proteins associated with prostate cancer risk years before diagnosis. These findings are not yet suitable for clinical screening.

Read the university research summary ↗
October 2026 · Further validation needed

Can genomic testing help tailor treatment?

UCL researchers reported that Decipher scores may help identify who benefits from more intensive treatment in certain high-risk cases. More validation is needed before routinely using it for that decision.

Read UCL's research summary ↗
July 2026 · FDA approval

A new option for certain advanced cancers

The FDA expanded approval of a PSMA-targeted radioligand treatment for eligible patients with metastatic prostate cancer. This is not a general screening or early-stage treatment.

Read the FDA announcement ↗
