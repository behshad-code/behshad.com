Research & updates

What's changing in prostate health?

Research brings new possibilities, but a promising study isn't the same as an approved test or treatment. These selected developments show what the evidence says today.

May 2026 · Professional guideline

Screening and biopsy guidance is evolving

The 2026 AUA/SUO amendment covers MRI, biomarkers, and initial and repeat biopsy strategy in risk-adapted screening and follow-up. Recommendations depend on personal risk and clinical context.

Review the guideline amendment ↗
August 2026 · Phase 3 trial

New high-risk surgery treatment data

In PROTEUS, adding apalutamide to ADT around prostatectomy improved five-year metastasis-free survival for selected high-risk patients, but serious adverse events were more common. This is not a low-risk treatment recommendation.

Read the peer-reviewed trial ↗
July 2026 · FDA approval

PSMA-targeted therapy for selected metastatic disease

The FDA expanded Pluvicto's indication with an androgen receptor pathway inhibitor for adults with PSMA-positive metastatic androgen pathway modulation-naïve or -sensitive prostate cancer. It does not apply to localized disease.

Read the FDA approval ↗
