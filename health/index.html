Our approach

Good information makes room for nuance.

Men’s Health Compass translates medical evidence into clear explanations, practical knowledge, and better questions, helping men understand their health and navigate decisions with their healthcare professionals.

Health decisions are personal. Our role is to explain the evidence and tradeoffs, not to tell readers what treatment to choose.

This publication is being built as a living educational library. New research will be considered in context, not treated as a breakthrough simply because it makes news.

Sources first

Link to primary research and established medical institutions wherever practical.

Clear about uncertainty

Distinguish established evidence from early findings and unanswered questions.

Careful updates

Identify publication and review dates when detailed articles are added or revised.

Readers decide with clinicians

Provide education, not personal medical advice or individualized diagnoses.
