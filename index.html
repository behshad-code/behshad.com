About

A technology leader at the intersection of innovation, security and enterprise value.

Some of the most interesting work in my career has happened when a business was at a turning point. Rapid growth, IPOs, acquisitions, private equity ownership, and major transformations bring difficult decisions about people, priorities, risk, and investment. Those are the challenges I enjoy working through.

I’ve worked in startups, high-growth companies, and global enterprises, serving as CIO and CISO and leading teams across technology strategy, cybersecurity, enterprise risk, business systems, and operations. Across different industries and stages of growth, I’ve approached technology as part of the larger business, with decisions grounded in what the organization needs to achieve.

My M&A experience spans technology due diligence before an acquisition and integration afterward. Bringing organizations together means aligning people, processes, and priorities as well as systems. I focus on understanding the investment objectives, identifying what needs to change, and helping the business capture the value it expects.

AI is opening another important chapter in that work. I’m interested in how it will reshape business models, productivity, and decision-making, and what it takes to turn that potential into lasting value. For executive teams, investors, and boards, AI strategy and governance need to connect business opportunity with cybersecurity, enterprise risk, sound economics, and human judgment.
